ADDITIONAL STRUCTURE DRAWINGS — Extra Molecular Structures in Image
====================================================================

The structure parser has identified that this image contains molecular structures

What this means for extraction:
- These are NOT part of the reaction arrow itself (they are not reactants or products
  of the main transformation), but they ARE chemically meaningful — they typically
  represent reagents, solvents, catalysts, ligands, or reference compounds that the
  table cells refer to by a short label or abbreviation.
- When you encounter a table cell value (e.g., "Solvent A", "L3", "ox-1") that matches

  chemical identity of that cell value.
- Do NOT guess or fabricate SMILES for these compounds — their structures are drawn
  explicitly in the image. Use your visual understanding to identify them.
- If a drawn structure has no visible label, it is still worth noting its approximate
  structure in 'additional_info' if it appears contextually relevant to a table row.
- These structures are also being resolved by the label registry system, which will
  supply verified SMILES after extraction. Your role is to correctly link cell values
  to their labels so the registry can match them.
